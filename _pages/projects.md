    <h2 class="archive__item-title"><a href="https://datascience.uni-greifswald.de/forschung/projects/behaive/">BehAIve</a></h2>
    <p class="page__meta"><i class="fas fa-fw fa-folder-open" aria-hidden="true"></i> European Union &middot; April 2026 &ndash; Present</p>
    <p>BehAIve (Behaviour Monitoring and Support of Older Adults) develops an AI-supported, situation-adaptive system that supports older adults in everyday life and geriatric patients during hospital stays. Continuous sensor monitoring is used to analyse behaviour and identify problematic patterns, with the aim of improving quality of life for people with cognitive impairments while reducing the workload of caregivers.</p>
    <ul>
      <li>My contributions: automatic knowledge extraction, multimodal behaviour analysis, and activity recognition, alongside project coordination and doctoral student co-supervision.</li>
      <li>Partners: University of Greifswald (Institute of Data Science), University of Rostock, University of Applied Sciences Wismar, German Centre for Neurodegenerative Diseases (DZNE), and Universit&auml;tsmedizin Greifswald.</li>
    </ul>

    <h2 class="archive__item-title"><a href="https://datascience.uni-greifswald.de/forschung/projects/dqatext/">DQAText</a></h2>
    <p class="page__meta"><i class="fas fa-fw fa-folder-open" aria-hidden="true"></i> German Research Foundation (DFG) &middot; October 2024 &ndash; March 2026</p>
    <p>DQAText (Assessing Data Quality Aspects of Texts) laid the groundwork for a fully integrated data quality assessment pipeline for text data in health research. It extended existing data quality frameworks with natural language processing methods for assessing structured, semi-structured, and unstructured text fields. The project was part of the TMF initiative on standards and tools for data monitoring in observational health studies.</p>
    <ul>
      <li>Developed a Python package for data quality assessment of ICD-10-GM, SNOMED CT, and OPS codes.</li>
      <li>Worked on an information retrieval task using large language models (LLMs) on histology texts collected at Universit&auml;tsmedizin Greifswald.</li>
      <li>Assessed the quality of online forum texts using a machine learning-based algorithm.</li>
    </ul>
    <p><a href="https://datascience.uni-greifswald.de/forschung/projects/dqatext/">View on Institute of Data Science</a></p>

    <h2 class="archive__item-title"><a href="https://datascience.uni-greifswald.de/forschung/projects/edem-connect/">eDEM-Connect</a></h2>
    <p class="page__meta"><i class="fas fa-fw fa-folder-open" aria-hidden="true"></i> Federal Ministry of Education and Research (BMBF) &middot; February 2020 &ndash; July 2023</p>
    <p>eDEM-Connect was a joint project to develop a communication and services platform for informal caregivers of people with dementia. The University of Greifswald sub-project developed, semi-automatically, the ontology holding the domain knowledge that a chatbot needs to give caregivers personalised support.</p>
    <ul>
      <li>Outcomes include the <a href="/research-artifacts/#edem-connectonto">eDEM-CONNECTONTO</a> agitation ontology and the ontology-based eDEM-Connect chatbot.</li>
      <li>Partners: Witten/Herdecke University (lead), University of Greifswald, Fraunhofer ISST, DZNE Witten, Lutheran University of Applied Sciences Nuremberg, Ergosign, and Youse.</li>
    </ul>
